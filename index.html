What you get

  • Answers from people who've done it

    Decades of experience on the exact kind of problem you're facing.

  • Fast and bounded

    One session is 45 minutes. You ask, you get a time slot, you move on.

  • Low-stakes

    Ask the question you'd hesitate to bring to your manager.

  • It goes both ways

    You can offer something in return, such as AI video editing, new tools or new trends. Teaching a little makes the exchange a conversation, not a favour.

  • A follow-up that matters

    30 days later, you note what changed thanks to the session.

  • A contact for the long run

    The expert stays in your network after the session.

What Relais is not

  • Not a replacement for your manager or your team.

  • Not a training course or a formal mentoring programme with homework.

  • Not a guarantee that someone says yes to every request: experts choose what they take on.

About us

We're a team of students working on Relais because experience leaves companies every year, and the people who stay often have to rediscover what the last generation already knew. We think a career shouldn't end with a handover and a farewell party. Relais is our way of testing whether it can continue, and we're building it with the people it's for.
